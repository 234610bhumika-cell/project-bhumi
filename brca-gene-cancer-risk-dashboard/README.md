# BRCA Gene & Cancer Risk Dashboard

**Tools:** Power BI, SQL, Public Genetic Databases
**Context:** Deshbandhu College, University of Delhi — DBT Star College Program
**Result:** 🏆 Ranked 1st out of 40+ entries

## Problem
Hereditary cancer risk data (BRCA1/BRCA2 mutation prevalence, cancer-type risk,
population-level baselines) is scattered across public genetic databases and hard
for non-technical stakeholders to interpret at a glance.

## Approach
- Consolidated 3+ public datasets into a single data pipeline feeding an interactive
  Power BI dashboard
- Applied data visualization and stakeholder-communication best practices to make
  complex genomic trend analysis readable for a non-technical audience
- Built out gene- and population-level breakdowns (carrier prevalence, risk ratio,
  risk elevation) with regional and family-history filters

## Key Views
- Average lifetime risk vs. general population risk, by cancer type
- Carrier prevalence vs. population baseline, by demographic group
- Mutation-positive rate by gene and region, with family-history filter

## Outcome
Recognized as the top submission (1st of 40+) in the DBT Star College Program for
converting complex genomic data into a clear, decision-ready visual narrative.
